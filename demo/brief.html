Prototype brief

Creator Revenue Copilot

Gumroad already shows creators what happened. Revenue Copilot tells them what to do next by turning product analytics into evidence-backed revenue actions.

What This Demo Proves

  • Products are loaded from seeded local demo data.
  • Metrics are computed deterministically before any recommendation text.
  • Signals are selected from views, sales, conversion, refunds, referrers, and metadata.
  • Every Copilot card cites the evidence used to support it.

Real Product Path

  1. Gumroad Rails models/database provide the facts.
  2. A metrics builder computes product performance.
  3. A signal detector finds actionable revenue patterns.
  4. An AI service packages approved signals into creator-facing JSON.
  5. The analytics UI renders reviewable next moves.

Data Boundary

This standalone version uses synthetic demo rows only. It does not scrape Gumroad, call Gumroad production services, read creator records, or make autonomous product changes.
